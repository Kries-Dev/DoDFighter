# DarthNox

`--mod mods/darthnox` adds **DarthNox** (record ID 61) to the character picker.

A musketeer with idle, walk, run, two sword attacks, a jump, hurt and knockdown, generated with AutoSprite
(character "DarthNox LF2 v4") and converted with `tools/autosprite_to_lf2.py`.

## Fighter profile

A **fast fencer with low defence and medium attack.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Speed | walk 6.0 / run 10.5 / dash 19 (Deep: 5 / 8.5 / 17) |
| Defence | takes 10% more damage (`armor` 91, set in `match.lua`) |
| Attack | sword slashes, 30 per hit (Deep's punches are 35), with sword reach and a weapon swish; specials below |

| Input | Move | Frames | MP | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D>A | Crescent | 235-238 | 60 | horizontal swing (sword swish) that launches a sword wave (`crescent.txt`, object 62) at ~11 px/tick: ~390 px, about half a screen; 21 damage, knocks down |
| DJA | Parry | 240-244 | 200 | ~1 s guard (241-243) with a blue aura. A hit landing in it is cancelled and he counters (245-248): an unblockable stab, 70 damage, leaves the victim dizzy. The counter has no hurtbox. |
| D>J | Lunge (D^A cancels it into the rising slash) | 260-263 | 75 | travelling stab (~125 px) with a sword sound and three fading afterimages; wide, tall blade box that hits every 5 ticks (11 each, ~4 hits), last ones knock down |
| D^A | Rising slash | 270-273 | 100 | invincible launch, then an upward sweep that launches (45 + 20); peaks at ~100 px whether it hits or not, then a plain fall (no hop) |
| DvA | Flurry | 280-288 | 50 | three heavier stabs (high, low, middle, 14 each, with a swish) that keep the opponent flinching on his feet, then a finisher that knocks down (22). Press A during the third stab for another round of three (20 MP); it ends when MP runs out |

All of this is generated by `tools/darthnox_moves.py` (tables at the top of the
file): edit it there and re-run, never hand-edit the generated part of
`darthnox.txt`. The parry counter is the one thing frame data can't express;
`combat.lua` does it. Verified in headless matches against Deep: each move
starts, hits, and knocks down / launches / stuns as listed.

## What is authored vs inherited

| | |
|---|---|
| Own frames (mechanics too) | normal attacks as sword slashes (60–62, 65–67, 70–73, jump attack 80–82, run attack 85–88), the roll (102–107), the special moves 235–287 |
| Own inputs | standing, walking and defend (110) carry his `hit_*` fields |
| Own pictures | standing (0–3), walking (5–8), running (9–11), attacks (60–62, 65–67), super attack (70–73), jump attack (80–82), run attack (85–88), jump (210–212, 215, 219), defend (110–111), hurt (220–229), falling (180–191), lying (230–231), air flips (100–101, 108–109), frozen (200–202), burning (203–207), grab and stab (120–123), throws (232–234, item throws 45–54), being held (130–144) |
| Inherited from Deep | every other frame's mechanics — states, `itr`/`bdy` boxes, next-wiring, catches, falls |

`object_data.lua` decides per frame: a frame in `darthnox.txt` with a `bdy:` or
`itr:`, or whose name differs from Deep's frame at that number, replaces Deep's
frame whole; a frame with `hit_*` fields swaps Deep's inputs; anything else
only swaps the picture. Frames kept from Deep that play LF2's punch sound
(`data\007.wav`: dash attack, grab throw; `014`: hitting a held opponent) play the
weapon swish (`008`) instead.

Every frame kept from Deep has its hitboxes, spawn, weapon and catch points
moved by the difference between Deep's centre and ours (`rebase` in
`object_data.lua`). All of those are measured from the frame centre, so without
the shift his inherited hitboxes sat ~34 px behind him (normal attacks missed,
hits landed on empty air, held weapons floated).

Unauthored frames fall back to `IDLE_PICTURE` (see `object_data.lua`), so the
fighter is playable immediately and unfinished actions read as a held pose
rather than a missing sprite. Add frames to `darthnox.txt` and they stop
falling back — no Lua changes needed.

Like the example character, this still needs the player's installer for the
base game's mechanics.

## Why `.txt` and not `.dat`

OpenLF2 decides by file extension whether to decrypt: `read_data_text` in
`src/resources/data_file.cpp` runs *any* path ending in `.dat` through the LF2
cipher unconditionally. Authored mod data is plaintext, so a `.dat` extension
makes the engine decrypt it into garbage and fail with
`NUL or 0x1A byte in data text`.

Keep authored character data as `.txt`. Only genuinely encrypted files from the
original game may use `.dat`.

## Sprite sheet

- `darthnox.bmp` — 484×1911, 84 cells of 120×90, 4 across × 21 down (`row: 4  col: 21`).
  Pictures 0–3 idle, 4–7 walk, 8–11 run, 12–14 slash, 16–18 thrust,
  20–22 jump (crouch, rise, air), 24–26 hurt (recoil, smaller recoil, recovering),
  28–31 knockdown (tipping, airborne, flat, lying), 32–34 crescent swing,
  36–38 parry guard, 40–42 power stab (counter), 44–46 lunge with afterimages,
  48–50 rising slash, 52–54 stab flurry (high, low, middle) with ghost blades, 56–59 roll, 60–61 frozen,
  64–66 burning, 68–71 grab (grip, hold, rapier raised, stab), 72–74 throw
  (overhead wind-up, hurl, follow-through), 76–78 parry stance with a blue aura
  (the plain 36–38 stay for blocking), 80–82 run-attack thrust with a
  glowing blade. Forward falls reuse
  the knockdown pictures until there is face-down art.
- `crescent.bmp` — the sword wave, 4×2 cells of 34×64 (flying, breaking up), drawn
  by `tools/make_crescent.py`. Stock fighters use
  79×79 cells and stand ~73px tall; DarthNox's tallest frame is 82px because
  the hat and plume sit on top of a head as big as Davis's.
- LF2 proportions are super-deformed: about 3 heads tall, head a third of the
  body, short broad torso, big gloves and boots. Earlier designs at ~5 heads
  (v1/v3) had heads far too small next to Davis, and a slim anime version (v2)
  read as a stick figure. Black hair, clean-shaven.
- Keep the rapier short and thick: a thin blade disappears at 74px, and
  AutoSprite's stock idle/walk dropped v2's thin blade entirely (custom clips
  describing the blade "fully visible in every frame" brought it back).
- **Every cell is followed by a 1px gutter**, like stock LF2 sheets (79px cells
  at an 80px pitch, 10×80 = 800 wide). `scripts/base/game/object_data.lua`
  addresses cell (c, r) at `x = c*(w+1)`, `y = r*(h+1)`, so a sheet must be at
  least `columns*(w+1) - 1` wide. A tightly packed 268px strip puts picture 3 at
  x 204–271, past the edge, and fails with `sprite rectangle outside texture`.
  Gutter pixels are palette index 0 (transparent). Note: `--headless` and
  `--input-trace` runs do not perform this check — only the windowed renderer does.
- **`row:` and `col:` are named backwards in the LF2 format.** `row:` is the
  number of pictures *per row* (columns) and `col:` is the number of rows —
  `object_data.cpp` reads them that way (`sheet.columns = require(..., "row:")`).
  A 4-across strip is therefore `row: 4  col: 1`.
- 8-bit indexed, uncompressed, bottom-up BITMAPINFOHEADER
- **Palette index 0 is pure black and reserved for transparency.** OpenLF2 keys
  on RGB(0,0,0) via `SDL_SetColorKey`, so the exporter lifts any opaque pixel
  that would quantise to pure black up to (8,8,8). DarthNox's near-black cape
  and hat make this necessary — without it, parts of him render as holes.
- `centerx: 71  centery: 89` — AutoSprite's foot pivot; every frame is registered
  on it, so one centre pair is right for all pictures

## Portraits

LF2 uses two different images, and they must be different sizes:

| key | file | size | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `head:` | `portrait_f.bmp` | 120×120 | character picker |
| `small:` | `portrait_s.bmp` | 40×45 | in-match HUD, beside the HP/MP bars |

Pointing both at one 80×80 image makes the HUD icon spill over the bars.
Both are cropped from the AutoSprite base image by `tools/autosprite_portrait.py`,
on a dark backdrop like the stock faces. `portrait.bmp` is the old design's
80×80 source, kept for `tools/resize_darthnox.py`.

## Scale

Built at 1× (LF2 native). `scripts/base/game/constants.lua` sets the world to
794×550 and upscales for display, so sprites must be authored in 79-unit space.
The 316px masters are kept upstream; see the pipeline notes for the 4K question.

## Style

The look is locked — see `STYLE.md` at the repo root before generating any
new art.

## Menu music

`music.lua` turns the menu music (`bgm/main.wma`, played on the title, character
select and options screens) off: menus are silent, match and stage music play
as normal. Set `MENU_MUSIC = true` in that file to bring it back.

## Mathilde (record ID 63)

A **jester dancer with a bo staff: the fastest in the cast, fragile, built for
combos.** Her data is `assets/mathilde.txt`, generated whole by
`tools/mathilde_moves.py`; her generic mechanics (getting hit, falls, grabs,
items) come from **Davis** (`object_data.lua` → `FIGHTERS`).

| | |
|---|---|
| Speed | walk 7.0 / run 13.5 / dash 21 (fastest in the main cast) |
| Defence | takes 25% more damage (`armor` 80, `match.lua`) |
| Attack | A, A, A chains swing → poke → overhead smash (22, 22, 40 + knockdown); short frames |

| Input | Move | Frames | MP | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## More than one fighter

Every fighter lives in this one mod. Adding one means: an entry in `catalog.lua`
(`fighters_added`, plus any objects its moves spawn), its stock base in
`object_data.lua` (`FIGHTERS`), its defence in `match.lua` (`DEFENCE`), and its
assets in `package.manifest`. Keep each asset path ≤ 29 characters.

## Urban Bridge stage

The mod also adds **Urban Bridge** to the Versus background list (after
Thunder Volcano; Random can pick it), with its own music.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `assets/bg/bridge.txt` | stage data: one layer, `width: 794`, `zboundary: 359 507`, HK Coliseum's shadow |
| `assets/bg/bridge.bmp` | the painting, 794×447, 8-bit, at `y: 103` |
| `assets/bg/gravity_stars.wma` | "Gravity Stars", WMA 8 |

- `catalog.lua` inserts the stage before the stock list's three unused templates,
  so the Random rule (skip the last two, third-last = Lee On Road) still holds.
- `camera.lua` puts invisible walls at x 60 and 734 on this stage only; the
  engine's own limits (0 and 794) let a fighter stand half off-screen on the
  concrete barriers.
- `match.lua` starts "Gravity Stars" when a match is on Urban Bridge, replacing
  the Versus music pick; Music OFF stays off, and the title demo keeps its music.
- The walkable band runs from just under the guardrail to just above the rubble
  at the front edge.
- Paths inside the bg file must be 29 characters or less (the original parser's
  buffer), hence the short `bg/bridge.*` names.
- Music must be WMA 8: OpenLF2's FFmpeg only has the `wmav2` decoder. See
  `STYLE.md` → Stages.

## Rebuilding

From AutoSprite (download each spritesheet's sheet PNG and atlas JSON). `walk2`/`run2`
are the clips made from the Idle Lock pose — the older `walk`/`run` have a smaller head:

```
python tools/autosprite_to_lf2.py out s0.345992 idle.png,idle.json,4 walk2.png,walk2.json,4,1,14 run2.png,run2.json,4,1,4 \
    slash.png,slash.json,@5/6/7 thrust.png,thrust.json,@3/5/12 jump.png,jump.json,@12/9/6,ground \
    hurt.png,hurt.json,@7/8/11 fall.png,fall.json,@7/9/11/14,ground \
    swing.png,swing.json,@3/4/12 parry.png,parry.json,@3/5/9 stab.png,stab.json,@2/4/13 \
    stab.png,stab.json,@3/4/8,trail rise.png,rise.json,@6/10/12,ground flurry2.png,flurry2.json,@7/5/10,blades \
    roll.png,roll.json,@4/6/9/11,ground hurt.png,hurt.json,@7/8,ice fall.png,fall.json,@7/9/11,ground,burn \
    grab.png,grab.json,@4/5/9/10 throw.png,throw.json,@9/10/12 parry.png,parry.json,@3/5/9,aura thrust.png,thrust.json,@3/5/12,bladeglow
python tools/make_crescent.py out/crescent.bmp
python tools/darthnox_moves.py mods/darthnox/assets
python tools/autosprite_portrait.py base.png out 130,60,730,660 230,170,610,598
python tools/image_to_stage.py "urban bridge stage.png" out "Urban Bridge" 540 850
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\to_wma.ps1 "Gravity stars.mp3" out\gravity_stars.wma
```

The first prints the `w/h/row/col/centerx/centery` values for `darthnox.txt`.
Add a row (e.g. `run.png,run.json,4`) to extend the sheet; pictures continue
row by row (row 2 = 8–11, ...). `s0.345992` is his locked scale — keep it the same across rebuilds so he
doesn't change size.

`tools/resize_darthnox.py` is the older tool that rescaled the first design's
67×59 sheet; it is kept for reference.
